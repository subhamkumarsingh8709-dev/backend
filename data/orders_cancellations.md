# Orders and Cancellations

## Cancel an order
You can cancel for free before the order is dispatched. Go to My Orders, select the order, and tap Cancel. Refunds for cancelled prepaid orders follow the normal refund timeline.

## Cancel after dispatch
Once an order is dispatched it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse delivery at the door or return the item after delivery under the 30 day return policy.

## Modify an order
Items, sizes, and quantities cannot be changed after the order is placed. Cancel the order before dispatch and place a new one.

## Order confirmation not received
Check your spam folder and the Orders section of the app. If the payment was successful but there is no order, the amount is reversed automatically within 5 to 7 business days.

## Partial deliveries
Orders with multiple items can arrive in separate packages. Each package has its own tracking link. Shipping is charged only once.

## Out of stock after ordering
If an item becomes unavailable after you order, we cancel it automatically and refund the amount within 5 to 7 business days.
