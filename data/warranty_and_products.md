# Warranty and Product Issues

## Warranty on electronics
Electronics carry the manufacturer's 1 year warranty. The invoice in My Orders is your proof of purchase. For warranty repairs, contact the brand's service center with the invoice.

## Dead on arrival
If an electronic product does not work when you open the box, report it within 7 days of delivery. We arrange a free pickup and send a replacement or refund after verification.

## Missing items or accessories
If a package arrives with an item or accessory missing, report it within 7 days with a photo of the package and the contents. Do not discard the packaging until the case is closed.

## Product different from the listing
If the product you received does not match the description or photos, raise a return for Wrong Item. We send a replacement or a full refund at no cost to you.

## Size guide
Each product page has a size chart. If the fit is wrong you can exchange within 30 days, subject to stock.
