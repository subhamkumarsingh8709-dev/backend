# App Troubleshooting and Contacting Support

## App crashes or does not open
Update the app to the latest version, restart your phone, and clear the app cache. If the problem continues, uninstall and reinstall the app. The app supports Android 9 and above and iOS 14 and above.

## Notifications not arriving
Make sure notifications are enabled for QuickCart in your phone settings and that battery saver is not restricting the app.

## Page keeps loading at checkout
Check your internet connection, close other apps, and try again. If the page still fails, try the website in a browser. Do not tap Pay repeatedly, as that can create duplicate payment attempts.

## Contact support
Chat support is available from 9 am to 9 pm IST every day inside the app under Help. You can also email support@quickcart.example and expect a reply within 24 hours. Keep your order ID ready.

## Escalations
If your issue is not resolved within 3 business days, ask for it to be escalated to a senior agent. Include your ticket ID in the message.
