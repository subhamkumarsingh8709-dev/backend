# Shipping and Delivery at QuickCart

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days in metro cities and 5 to 8 business days in remote areas. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and is available in selected pin codes.

## Shipping charges
Standard shipping is free on orders above Rs 999. Below that, a shipping fee of Rs 49 applies. Express shipping costs Rs 99 on every order.

## Tracking your order
Open My Orders in the app or website and tap Track Order. You also receive an SMS and email with a tracking link once the order is dispatched. Tracking updates can take up to 12 hours to appear after dispatch.

## Delayed or stuck orders
If an order has not moved for 3 business days or is more than 2 business days late, contact support with your order ID. We raise a trace with the courier, and if the package is lost we send a replacement or refund in full.

## Wrong address
The address can be changed only before the order is dispatched, from My Orders. After dispatch, contact support immediately and we will try to redirect the package, but this is not guaranteed.
