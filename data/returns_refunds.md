# Returns and Refunds at QuickCart

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with all tags and accessories.

## Non-returnable items
Innerwear, personalized items, gift cards, and perishable goods cannot be returned. Items with a manufacturing defect or the wrong item delivered can always be returned or replaced.

## How to start a return
Go to My Orders, select the item, and tap Return or Exchange. Choose a reason and a pickup slot. Pickup is free, and our courier collects the item from your address within 2 to 3 business days.

## Exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different size or color within 30 days, subject to stock. If the size is not available, we issue a refund instead.

## Refund timeline
Once the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection, which takes up to 2 business days, the refund is started. Refunds to cards and netbanking take 5 to 7 business days. UPI refunds take 1 to 3 business days. Refunds to QuickCart Wallet are instant.

## Refunds for Cash on Delivery orders
For COD orders, refunds go to the QuickCart Wallet instantly, or to your bank account if you share the details in the Refund Method section of the return request. Bank transfers take 3 to 5 business days.

## Damaged or wrong items
Report damaged, defective, or wrong items within 7 days of delivery with photos of the product and the packaging. We arrange a free pickup and send a replacement or a full refund.
