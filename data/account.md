# Your QuickCart Account

## Reset your password
On the login screen tap Forgot Password, enter your registered email or phone number, and use the OTP or reset link we send. The link expires in 15 minutes.

## Not receiving the OTP
Check that your phone has network coverage and that your number is correct. Wait 60 seconds before requesting a new OTP. Messages can be delayed if DND is on for promotional SMS, so try the email option instead.

## Change email or phone number
Go to Profile, then Account Settings, and choose Edit. You must verify the new email or number with an OTP before the change takes effect.

## Two-factor authentication
Turn on two-factor authentication under Profile, Security. You will need an OTP in addition to your password at each new login.

## Delete your account
Go to Settings, Privacy, and choose Delete Account. Deletion is completed within 7 days. Pending orders and wallet balance must be cleared first, and deletion cannot be undone.

## Account locked
After 5 wrong password attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. You can reset your password to unlock it sooner.
