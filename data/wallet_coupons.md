# Wallet, Coupons and Offers

## QuickCart Wallet
The wallet stores refunds and cashback and can be used at checkout for any order. Wallet balance does not expire for 12 months from the date it was credited. It cannot be withdrawn to a bank account.

## Using a coupon
Enter the code in the Apply Coupon box at checkout. Only one coupon can be used per order, and each coupon has a minimum cart value and an expiry date shown in its terms. A coupon can be used together with wallet balance.

## Coupon not working
Check the minimum cart value, the expiry date, and whether the offer applies to the items in your cart. Some categories such as gift cards are excluded from offers.

## Cashback
Cashback is credited to your wallet within 48 hours after the order is delivered. It is reversed if the order is returned.

## Expired coupons
Expired coupons cannot be restored or extended. A coupon used on an order that is later cancelled is returned to your account if it has not expired.
