# Payments at QuickCart

## Accepted payment methods
We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, netbanking, QuickCart Wallet, and Cash on Delivery. Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to Rs 10,000 and has a handling fee of Rs 40.

## Payment failed but money was deducted
If your payment fails but the amount is debited, it is automatically reversed to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. You do not need to place the order again to get the money back. If the amount is not reversed after 7 business days, share the transaction ID with support.

## EMI options
No-cost and standard EMI are available on selected credit cards for orders above Rs 3,000. EMI options appear at checkout when your card is eligible.

## Duplicate charges
If you were charged twice for one order, the extra amount is refunded automatically within 5 to 7 business days. Contact support with both transaction IDs if it is not reversed after that.

## Invoices
Your GST invoice is available in My Orders under Download Invoice once the order is delivered. It is also emailed to you.
